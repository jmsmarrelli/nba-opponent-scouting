# Scouting methods

The two pinned archives provide 2025–26 regular-season shots and play-by-play. A game must have unique chronological event IDs, complete period clocks, consistent shot/event links on scorer, value and outcome, monotone score progression without implausible jumps and score reconciliation from field goals plus made free throws. The audit retains 1,212 of 1,230 games and exports exclusions.

Within those games, player rebounds are classified by comparing the rebounding team with the last missed-shot team in the period. Team rebounds are omitted. Opponent defensive rebounds are joined within each game before aggregation; using the offense’s own defensive rebounds would be the wrong denominator.

Rates are ratios of season totals, not averages of game percentages. The league location benchmark pools attempts. Offensive and defensive panels use the same audited games for each team. Period lengths include overtime when calculating estimated pace. All figures are historical and unadjusted for opponents, availability or score context.

Source: [pinned public NBA archive](https://github.com/shufinskiy/nba_data/tree/e829d4678be1e075f99e5d41a1c5f97089be446b). No possession is represented as manually watched or tagged.
